## 📖 What is WhisperDeck?

WhisperDeck is a free, easy-to-use audio transcription tool that runs entirely on your computer. It lets you convert voice recordings, interviews, lectures, or any audio file into written text - **without needing an internet connection** and **without sending your audio anywhere else**.

Think of it as your personal, private transcription assistant. You load an audio file, WhisperDeck listens to it, and gives you back a clean text document. All processing happens locally on your device, so your conversations stay completely private.

## 🎯 How to Use WhisperDeck Effectively

### Transcribing a Recording

1. Click **File** → **Open** and choose your audio file.
2. Select the language of the audio from the dropdown menu (or set it to "Auto-detect").
3. Click the **Play** button to preview the audio if you want to verify it's correct.
4. Click **Transcribe** and wait for the process to finish.
5. Use the **Edit** tools to correct any minor errors in the text.

### Trimming Audio

If your recording has long silences or irrelevant parts:

1. Click the **Trim** tab.
2. Use the timeline to select the start and end points you want to keep.
3. Click **Apply Trim** to create a new, shorter audio file.
4. Then transcribe the trimmed version for faster results.

### Tagging Your Files

Keep your transcriptions organized:

1. After transcription, click the **Tags** field.
2. Type keywords like "meeting", "interview", "personal", or "lecture".
3. Press Enter to save the tag.
4. Use the **Search** bar at the top to find files by tag later.

### Exporting Your Work

1. Click **File** → **Export**.
2. Choose your format: TXT (plain text), DOCX (Word), or PDF.
3. Select a destination folder and click **Save**.

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

### Is WhisperDeck really free?

Yes, WhisperDeck is completely free and open-source. You can use it for personal or commercial projects without paying anything.

### Will my audio be uploaded anywhere?

No. WhisperDeck processes everything locally on your device. There is no cloud component, no analytics, and no data collection. Your files stay on your computer.

### What languages does it support?

WhisperDeck supports over 90 languages, including English, Spanish, French, German, Chinese, Japanese, Arabic, Hindi, and many more. It can also auto-detect the language if you're not sure.

### How accurate is the transcription?

Accuracy depends on audio quality and clarity. In good conditions (clear speech, minimal background noise), accuracy can be above 95%. For noisy recordings, expect around 85-90%.

### Can I use WhisperDeck for long recordings?

Yes, there's no strict length limit. However, very long files (over 2 hours) may take a while to process. We recommend trimming to shorter segments for faster results.

### What if I encounter an error?

Check that your audio file isn't corrupted and is in a supported format. If problems persist, visit the GitHub page and look under the "Issues" tab for solutions or to report a bug.

## 🔧 Troubleshooting

### WhisperDeck won't start

- Make sure you have enough free disk space (at least 2 GB).
- Try running the program as administrator (right-click → "Run as administrator").
- Check if your antivirus is blocking it. If so, add an exception for WhisperDeck.

### Transcription is very slow

- Close other heavy programs while transcribing.
- Use shorter audio segments (under 30 minutes works best).
- Try the "Fast" mode in settings for quicker but slightly less accurate results.

### No sound or audio errors

- Ensure your audio file isn't corrupted.
- Try converting the file to WAV format using a free tool like Audacity.
